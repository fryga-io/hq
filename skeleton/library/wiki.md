# Library Wiki

Compiled understanding of the external signals the company consumes. Each section
synthesizes what its items collectively tell us — patterns, connections, and
implications for our work. Individual library files hold the raw content; this
wiki is the understanding layer. The thin file listing lives in [[library/index]].

Every library item appears here and in [[library/index]]. The index is a one-line
pointer; this wiki is the multi-sentence analysis with cross-references.
